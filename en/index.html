How we get to this number

It is not a figure lifted from a foreign article. These are market ranges practised in Portugal, item by item.

01

24 items, not an average

From the venue to the rings, each item has a minimum, a typical value and a maximum. Some are fixed prices, others are charged per guest.

02

Adjusted to your area and date

Items that depend on the local market go up or down with the district and the time of year. Retail ones — dress, suit, rings — keep a national price.

03

Reviewed by people who organise weddings

The ranges are reviewed by wedding planning professionals. They are indicative: the final price depends on the suppliers chosen and on the date.

Questions we always get

Is this really free?

Simulating is free and needs no sign-up. We only ask for contact details from those who want the detailed budget and supplier proposals. Our partners pay us, never the couple.

Where do these prices come from?

From ranges practised in Portugal, organised by item and reviewed by wedding planning professionals. They are indicative: the final price always depends on the suppliers chosen and on the date.

Who ends up with our details?

Only whoever you allow. There are two separate permissions: one for our team to contact you, another for us to share the request with partner suppliers in your area. You can give only the first, and withdraw either at any time.

How far ahead should we start planning?

In-demand venues book high-season Saturdays 12 to 18 months ahead, and the date shapes almost everything else. Simulating early is mostly about not booking the venue before you know what is left for the rest.
